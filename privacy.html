Privacy Policy

Declassified Studio · Effective date: 2026-11-02

1. About this app

Declassified Studio is a personal desktop application used by a single creator to prepare short videos and to publish them to that creator's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Data we handle

  • TikTok OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) and the account's basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), obtained when the creator signs in with TikTok Login Kit.
  • Metrics of the creator's own videos (such as views, likes, comments and shares) read through the TikTok Display API.
  • Videos and captions that the creator chooses to upload or publish to their own account.
  • Content inputs sent to third-party providers in order to generate the creator's own videos (see section 4).
  • API keys for those providers.

The app does not collect data about other TikTok users, and does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

3. Where data is stored

Project data is stored locally on the creator's own computer. OAuth tokens and API keys are stored locally, in the Windows Credential Manager or in a local secrets file on that computer. We operate no server and receive none of this data. Apart from TikTok and the third-party providers listed in section 4, which receive data only to perform the actions the creator requests, no data is shared, sold or disclosed to anyone.

4. Third-party services

To generate the creator's own videos, the app sends the following data to these providers. Each provider processes that data under its own terms and privacy policy.

  • ElevenLabs receives script/narration text, to generate voice-over audio. Privacy policy · Terms
  • Kling AI receives prompts and reference images, to generate video clips and images. Privacy policy · Terms
  • Google Gemini API receives script and prompt text and stock frames, to write and analyze content. Privacy policy · Terms
  • Pixabay, Pexels and Coverr receive search queries, and the app downloads stock clips from them, to find footage. Pixabay: Privacy · Terms. Pexels: Privacy · Terms. Coverr: Privacy · Terms
  • vidIQ receives short topic search terms, to read public data about which kinds of videos perform well. No TikTok user data is sent to it. Privacy policy · Terms
  • ChatGPT (OpenAI), through the creator's own ChatGPT account, receives image descriptions, to create thumbnails and channel images. Privacy policy · Terms

None of these providers receives TikTok tokens or data read from TikTok.

TikTok itself receives only what the creator uploads or publishes, and provides the creator's own metrics.

5. How the data is used

Tokens are used solely to upload or publish the creator's videos and to read the creator's own video metrics. Metrics are used only to show the creator how their own content performs.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the creator's computer until the creator deletes it. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all locally stored projects and data; removing its entries from the Windows Credential Manager (and deleting the local secrets file) removes tokens and keys. Data already sent to the providers in section 4 is governed by their own policies.

7. Revoking access

The creator can revoke access at any time by (a) using the disconnect option in the app, which deletes the stored tokens, or (b) removing the app in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services (or the equivalent screen in the TikTok app). After revocation the app can no longer access the account.
